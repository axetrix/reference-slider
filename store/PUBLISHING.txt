REMAINING BEFORE SUBMISSION
1. Test version 1.2.1 on live Pinterest while signed in: feed, board, search, open pin, loading more pins, image details/source links, and local collections after a real browser restart. Automated tests use controlled pages and mocked Chrome storage, so they do not verify Pinterest's current live HTML or an installed extension service worker.
2. Register/sign into the Chrome Web Store developer dashboard and complete the account's registration, payment and verification requirements.
3. Use https://axetrix.github.io/reference-slider/ as the Privacy policy URL.
4. Set your actual developer/support contact information in the dashboard. The privacy policy refers users to that contact.
5. Upload the ZIP, icon, promo and screenshots. Paste listing text and permission justifications.
6. Complete the privacy/data-use declarations to match the code and privacy policy.
7. Review the store preview and submit for review when ready. Approval is determined by the store.
